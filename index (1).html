What We Do
Our Services

Full building and maintenance across Stoke-on-Trent, Staffordshire and Cheshire. Multi-trade, fully experienced, no job too big or small.

Extensions & New Builds

Single and double storey extensions, garden rooms and outbuildings built to the highest standard from groundworks to final finish.

Kitchens

Full kitchen design and installation including electrics, plumbing, tiling, plastering and fitting. Transforming kitchens from start to finish.

Bathrooms

Complete bathroom renovations including tiling, plumbing, plastering and fitting. Modern, stylish results delivered on time.

Roofing

Slate and tile roofing, flat roofs, soffits, fascias, guttering and drainage. Full roof replacements and repairs across Staffordshire.

Plastering

Skimming, rendering and full plastering for new builds, renovations and repairs. Smooth, lasting finishes throughout.

Fencing & Gates

Bespoke wooden fencing, side gates and boundary solutions. Built to last with a quality finish tailored to your property.

Landscaping & Gardens

Patios, driveways, lawns, garden buildings and full landscaping. Creating beautiful outdoor spaces from concept to completion.

Windows, Gutters & Soffits

Roof window installation, tile and slate repairs, replacement guttering and soffit work. Keeping your property weathertight and looking its best from top to bottom.
